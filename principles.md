# KLM Principles

## Overview
This document describes a layered system that translates brand values to experience principles, and experience principles to design principles. Keep them in mind at all times to provide an experience that is on line with our brand.

---

## Brand Values (the core)

- Passionate: Our love for flying, connecting people, and KLM unites us. It’s what binds us together and motivates us, no matter how challenging the situation. This passion energizes us every day, and is felt by the people around us.
- Innovative: Our love for flying, connecting people, and KLM unites us. It’s what binds us together and motivates us, no matter how challenging the situation. This passion energizes us every day, and is felt by the people around us.
- Caring: Taking good care of our customers and each other defines who we are. We touch people’s hearts by doing the expected and unexpected. We care for our colleagues by working together as one inclusive team and helping each other out.
- Responsible: We are transparent about our impact on the planet and embrace our role in society. We are committed to take action today towards more sustainable aviation. When making decisions, no matter how small, we consider the interests of all stakeholders.


---


## Experience principles (translation to interactions)

- We’re human and we serve humans, not passengers
- We’re genuine and true
- We’re giving moments of wonder and delight
- We’re here for everyone and treat every person equally
- We’re honest, even if the message if unfavorable


---

## Design principles (for making choices in visual identity, tome of voice, motion and product design)

- Warm: We’re empathetic and caring. Our designs feel soft, calm, and welcoming.
- Optimistic: We’re all for positivity, and we make you feel happy. Our designs evoke confidence and optimism without losing realism.
- Approachable: We’re down-to-earth and welcoming. Everything we create feels open, inviting, and inclusive.
- Informal: We’re human and speak easy-to-understand language. We avoid jargon and speak like people, not like systems.
- Genuine: We’re genuine and we keep it real. We communicate clearly and honestly, without exaggeration.
- Casual: We make you smile but know when we need to show authority. We balance friendliness with professionalism — light where possible, clear where needed.
